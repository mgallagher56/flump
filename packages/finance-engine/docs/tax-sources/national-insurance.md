Annual blended derivation: the sources give only per-period rates, except that E2022 states whole-year figures for directors (12.73 / 2.73 / 14.53, and 11,908). Arithmetic check: tax months 1-7 (6 Apr to 5 Nov) at 13.25 and months 8-12 at 12 gives (7*13.25 + 5*12)/12 = 12.73. Likewise (7*3.25+5*2)/12 = 2.73 and (7*15.05+5*13.8)/12 = 14.53. The directors figures are therefore the official annual-equivalent for evenly paid earnings. For ordinary employees the engine should either use period rates by pay date or use these blended values; the sources say nothing about a blended primary threshold other than the directors' 11,908 (a tax-year-weighted average of 9,880 and 12,570: 3 months at 9,880 and 9 at 12,570 gives 11,897.5, NOT 11,908, so 11,908 is HMRC's own annual method and should be used as stated).
