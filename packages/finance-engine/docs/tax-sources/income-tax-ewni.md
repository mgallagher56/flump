Logic observations (not table-value mismatches; reported because they conflict with VERIFIED rules):

1. tax-calculator.ts:487-491 computes PA taper from `totalGrossIncome` (gross, no deduction for gross pension contributions or grossed-up Gift Aid). Verified rule [ANI]: taper is driven by adjusted net income. Repo: gross income; verified: ANI. uk-tax.ts:376-379 does compute ANI (minus pension and 1.25x Gift Aid), so the two modules disagree.
2. tax-calculator.ts:503 `higherRateLimit === 125140 ? 50270 : 50000` hardcodes by magic number and treats 2021/22 and 2022/23 (higher threshold 50,270, additional 150,000) as 50,000 for the PSA higher-rate boundary. Verified: 2021/22 and 2022/23 higher rate starts at 50,271 (so threshold 50,270). Repo: 50,000 for those years; verified: 50,270.
3. tax-calculator.ts:639 and 657 derive higher band max taxable via `personalAllowance === 12500 ? 12500 : 12570`; with 150,000 additional threshold in 2021/22 and 2022/23 this is consistent, but the derivation is tapered-PA-unaware.
4. uk-tax.ts:200 hardcodes basic band 37,700 and uk-tax.ts:208-219 hardcode 50,270 and 125,140 (2023/24 onward values), with Gift Aid extension applied to both higher-rate start and additional threshold (uk-tax.ts:208, 219). This matches [S414] (both limits raised) for 2023/24 onward but is wrong for 2020/21 to 2022/23 (additional threshold 150,000; 2020/21 PA 12,500, basic width 37,500).
