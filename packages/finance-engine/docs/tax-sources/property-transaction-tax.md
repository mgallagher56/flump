# UK Property Transaction Taxes (E39 S1b, MA7)

Primary sources read on 2026-11-21 and re-read on 2026-11-22 (fable-decider sign-off) for main residential property purchases where the buyer(s) own no other home. Higher rates for additional dwellings and non-UK resident surcharges are outside version one.

## England & Northern Ireland — Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT)

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/stamp-duty-land-tax/residential-property-rates>
- Standard residential rates (from 1 April 2025):
  - Up to £125,000: 0%
  - £125,001 to £250,000: 2%
  - £250,001 to £925,000: 5%
  - £925,001 to £1,500,000: 10%
  - Over £1,500,000: 12%
- First-time buyer relief (purchases up to £500,000):
  - Up to £300,000: 0%
  - £300,001 to £500,000: 5%
  - If the purchase price is over £500,000, first-time buyer relief does not apply and standard residential rates apply to the whole price.

## Scotland — Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (LBTT)

- Source: <https://www.revenue.scot/taxes/land-buildings-transaction-tax/residential-property>
- Standard residential rates (from 1 April 2021):
  - Up to £145,000: 0%
  - £145,001 to £250,000: 2%
  - £250,001 to £325,000: 5%
  - £325,001 to £750,000: 10%
  - Over £750,000: 12%
- First-time buyer relief (no upper price cap):
  - Raises the 0% nil-rate threshold from £145,000 to £175,000 (maximum saving £600).
  - £175,001 to £250,000: 2%
  - £250,001 to £325,000: 5%
  - £325,001 to £750,000: 10%
  - Over £750,000: 12%

## Wales — Land Transaction Tax (LTT)

- Source: <https://www.gov.wales/land-transaction-tax-rates-and-bands>
- Main residential rates (from 10 October 2022):
  - Up to £225,000: 0%
  - £225,001 to £400,000: 6%
  - £400,001 to £750,000: 7.5%
  - £750,001 to £1,500,000: 10%
  - Over £1,500,000: 12%
- First-time buyer relief: none in Wales.
