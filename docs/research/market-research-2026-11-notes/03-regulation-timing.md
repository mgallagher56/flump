## Constraints on Flump (inferred)

- Stay on the guidance side: use the user's own inputs, show scenarios, avoid specific product recommendations or "you should" language for investments, pensions or mortgages. Standard line: "Guidance and projections, not regulated financial advice."
- No bank OAuth yet: when added, use an aggregator, with a 90-day reconfirm flow.
- Targeted support is a regulated activity. Do not simulate it with segment-based "people like you should..." nudges without FCA permission.
- MTD submission needs HMRC software recognition. Treat as a roadmap decision, timed against April 2027.
- Version all tax rules by tax year and keep a source URL per rule.
- Get a perimeter opinion from a lawyer before launch of anything that recommends. The FCA perimeter review has not been done.
