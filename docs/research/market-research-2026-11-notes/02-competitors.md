# Strand 2: Competitor landscape (checked 2026-11-12)

Method note: WebSearch only (search snippets, with a few failed fetches, e.g. moneytothemasses returned 403). Most pricing comes from affiliate/aggregator blogs that disagree with each other. Everything marked (U) is unverified and needs checking on the vendor page. Ratings are quoted only where a source showed them. I did not read Reddit or app-store pages directly, so recurring-complaint evidence is thin.

## Headline findings

1. **The UK aggregator-style field has thinned.** Money Dashboard closed 31 Oct 2023 (ClearScore, its owner since early 2022, shut it). Moneyhub reportedly closed its consumer app to new customers and shuts for existing users on 14 Aug 2026 (U: single source, Money to the Masses via search snippet; the article could not be fetched). Both leave a gap for UK "see everything in one place" users.
2. **MCP/LLM access is now a feature, not a differentiator, in the US** but mostly via unofficial or read-only routes. Copilot Money: read-only MCP beta (Dec 2025, secondary source) and an in-app Money Assistant (public beta, reportedly Apr 2026). Monarch: first-party AI Assistant (Winter Release, 18 Dec 2025) but no official MCP, only community servers using stored login credentials. YNAB: community MCP servers only. Emma (UK): its own plan page says you can connect it to Claude or ChatGPT, read-only.
3. **MTD for Income Tax began 6 Apr 2026** (over £50k qualifying income; £30k from Apr 2027; £20k from Apr 2028). This pushes sole traders towards paid bookkeeping software. HMRC listed 159 compatible products as of 20 Oct 2026 (U: one blog).

## Per-competitor notes

**Emma.** Largest UK budgeting app, tiered. Pricing from Emma's help centre and review sites: https://help.emma-app.com/en/article/1ywhulq/ and https://www.householdmoneysaving.com/emma-app-review/. Strengths: UK bank coverage, subscription detection, Claude/ChatGPT connection (https://emma-app.com/plans/compare-emma-plans). Complaints: price steep for a budgeting app and repeated upsell to Ultimate (householdmoneysaving, 2026). Trustpilot Feb 2026 mixed: one 5/5, one reviewer felt cheated over a charge (https://fi.trustpilot.com/review/emma-app.com). Aggregate score not found. Emma publicly positioned itself as the destination after Money Dashboard closed: https://emma-app.com/blog/now-that-money-dashboard-has-closed-what-are-my-budgeting-app-alternatives (2023).

**Snoop.** Freemium; Apple App Store 4.6/5 from 6.8K ratings (apppricinglab listing, 2026). Snoop courted Money Dashboard users with a premium trial. Little Trustpilot presence (profile apparently unclaimed). Sources: https://www.householdmoneysaving.com/snoop-app-review/, https://www.finder.com/uk/digital-banking/snoop-app-review.

**Moneyhub.** Trustpilot 2.5/5 from 66 reviews (ca.trustpilot.com, 2026) or 3.1 from about 60 (Money to the Masses). One Feb 2026 reviewer, a business customer, complained of canned AI replies. Consumer app closure Aug 2026 (U): https://moneytothemasses.com/news/best-moneyhub-alternatives/amp and https://www.householdmoneysaving.com/?p=16845. Still sold B2B (sales-led).

**Money Dashboard.** Shut after ClearScore acquisition. Users could export CSV until 31 Oct 2023 only. Sources: https://yourmoney.com/news/money-dashboard-to-close-at-the-end-of-october-what-users-need-to-know, https://moneytothemasses.com/news/money-dashboard-to-close-all-accounts-from-31st-october-2023. Where users went: Snoop, Emma, Moneyhub (now closing), Empower for investors. Lesson: userbase is sensitive to export and portability, and to vendor shutdown risk.

**Plum / Chip / Moneybox.** Savings and investing automation, not planning. Plum Trustpilot 4.0 from 9,200+ reviews and Moneybox 4.4 from 4,500+ (July 2026 comparison, via moneytothemasses investing apps page). Plum fees: Plus £3.99/mo (https://www.householdmoneysaving.com/plum-review/). Chip: no Trustpilot data found.

**Monzo / Starling / Chase.** Categories, pots and bills within their own bank only. Monzo generally judged strongest for budgeting (https://uktaxdrag.co.uk/monzo-vs-starling-vs-chase-uk-2026, affiliate). Pensions/net worth coverage not found.

**Taxfix UK (TaxScouts) and Pie.** Done-for-you or guided self assessment, annual and transactional. Taxfix Trustpilot has about 7,150 reviews, mixed with delay and extra-charge complaints (https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/taxfix.com/en-uk; score not seen). Pie from £39, vendor's own page https://www.pie.tax/tax-pible/pie-tax-vs-taxscouts-comparison. Neither does year-round planning.

**FreeAgent / Xero / Coconut.** MTD-ready bookkeeping. Prices from affiliate comparisons: https://dev.to/landolio/mtd-compatible-software-compared-what-uk-sole-traders-actually-need-not-the-ps30mo-ones-121h, https://whito.co.uk/tools/mtd-income-tax-software-cost-uk/. Xero said to have risen Ignite £16 to £18 in 2026 (U). All priced as business tools; none sells "take-home and tax across your whole life".

**YNAB.** Behaviour-change budgeting; US-centric; about $109/yr. MCP: community only (e.g. https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/issmirnov/ynab-mcp-server; one has 47 tools, read-only by default).

**Monarch.** $99.99/yr (U). AI Assistant asks before changing anything (https://help.monarch.com/hc/en-us/articles/37526856682260-AI-in-Monarch); Winter Release 18 Dec 2025 (https://www.monarch.com/blog/winter-release). No official API/MCP; community servers need email, password and TOTP secret (https://truthifi.com/mcp/monarch-money-mcp, June 2026).

**Copilot Money.** Apple-only, about $95/yr (U). Money Assistant beta (https://www.copilot.money/dispatch/beta-introducing-your-money-assistant); approval before edits. MCP beta read-only (secondary source: https://yespress.io/copilot-money).

**ProjectionLab.** Closest to Flump's planning axis: UK tax model plus UFPLS/PCLS withdrawal options (https://blog.projectionlab.com/pricing; https://projectionlab.com/changelog). Basic free, Premium $129/yr. Does not do ledger/self-employed.

**Boldin / Empower / Kubera.** US-first retirement or wealth dashboards; no UK evidence found. Kubera pricing too inconsistent to quote (https://toolradar.com/tools/kubera/pricing says about $20.83/mo billed yearly).

**Sharesight.** Investment tracker; UK CGT report not available per its own help (https://help.sharesight.com/uk/plans-overview/); UK pricing via https://sharesight.com/uk/pricing.

## Gap analysis: UK tax-aware planning

What the evidence suggests nobody does well together (inference from the above, not proven absence):

1. **Whole-life take-home and marginal-rate planning.** UK budgeters (Emma, Snoop) ignore tax; tax tools (Taxfix, Pie, FreeAgent, Coconut) file returns and do not model pension, salary sacrifice, student loan, HICBC, personal-allowance taper, or Scottish bands against actual balances.
2. **Joined-up pension, mortgage, debt and savings in GBP.** ProjectionLab models UK tax but is a scenario sandbox with no ledger. UK banks and robo apps see only their own wrappers.
3. **Self-employed and rental ledger linked to personal net worth and tax-year position.** MTD products are business accounting; none shows "what is left after tax, payments on account included".
4. **Portability and shutdown resilience.** Money Dashboard and Moneyhub consumer closures show demand for manual-first, exportable data that survives vendor failure; Flump's manual-first posture avoids Open Banking dependency.
5. **First-party, scoped, UK-aware MCP.** Existing MCP routes are unofficial (Monarch, YNAB) or read-only betas (Copilot, Emma). An official MCP with tax-year and allowance context, and clear read/write scopes, appears open.
6. **Free/low price anchor.** UK paid tiers cluster at about £4 to £15/mo; MTD software about £15 to £35/mo.
