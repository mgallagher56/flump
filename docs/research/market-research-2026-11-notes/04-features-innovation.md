## Key lessons from closures

- Mint (2024): migration to Credit Karma was lossy per several sources, which disagree on detail; users who skipped export had to rebuild. Lesson: self-serve full export and no ad-funded model. https://mymoneyblog.com/mint-to-credit-karma-transition-alternatives.html (n/s)
- Money Dashboard (31 Oct 2023): data access ended at close; a forum user said no replacement matched its spending-analysis dashboard. https://forums.moneysavingexpert.com/discussion/6476542/money-dashboard-apps-closing-down (2023)
- Moneyhub (2025-26): consumer app wound down to pursue B2B; third closure in UK consumer aggregation after Yolt. https://tech.eu/2025/02/14/moneyhub-scraps-d2c-app-over-30-jobs-reported-to-be-axed/ (2025-02-14)

## Implications for Flump (inference, not sourced)

1. Highest-confidence angles: export/ownership guarantee, no bank-sync fragility, transparent pricing.
2. UK tax-aware what-if (ANI thresholds, salary sacrifice, HICBC, student loan) is a clear whitespace versus all budgeting apps, though demand is inferred from content volume rather than request boards.
3. MCP is supply-rich but unproven demand; position as user-owned, scoped, read-first access and avoid regulated-advice language (FCA warning about AI advice protections).
4. Follow-ups needed: direct Reddit/Trustpilot/App Store mining (blocked here), self-employed and mortgage demand, couples in UK.
