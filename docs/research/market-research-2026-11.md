# Flump market research, November 2026

Researched 12 November 2026 from public web sources only (WebSearch and WebFetch). Purpose: evidence of customer demand, the features customers want, and innovations Flump can bring to market, to feed new epics.

**How to read the evidence.** Each claim carries a URL and a date. Grades:

- **V** = the page was opened and read.
- **S** = secondary (search summary, trade press, affiliate or vendor blog).
- **UNVERIFIED** = could not be confirmed on a primary source; do not encode in product without checking.

**Known limits of this research (read before relying on it):**

- Reddit, the App Store, Google Play and Trustpilot could not be fetched directly. Reddit appears only through third-party mirrors. Complaint mining from those sources is a follow-up, not done.
- No Google Trends, Ahrefs or Semrush volumes for the specific terms (£100k trap, Plan 5, salary sacrifice, Section 24, child benefit charge). The only volume data is one vendor study.
- Competitor prices mix official pages and affiliate blogs and sometimes conflict. Several competitors were not covered (Lunch Money, Actual, Voyant, Rocket Money, Ghostfolio, Wealthfolio, Yolt, QuickBooks SE, the HMRC app).
- Demand for self-employed tax pots and for mortgage or remortgage planning was **not found**. Absence of evidence here is not evidence of absence.
- Autumn Budget 2026 (28 October 2026) details come from press only and sources conflict. They are not used as evidence for any ranking below.

Context: what Flump already does and plans is in [pages_and_features.md](../pages_and_features.md), [app_functionality.md](../app_functionality.md) and [audit/EPICS.md](../audit/EPICS.md) (E1 to E24). Epic numbers proposed here (E25 to E29) are provisional; renumber if they collide at merge.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **The strongest, best-evidenced demand is the UK marginal-rate and take-home cluster.** HMRC-derived figures put 698,000 taxpayers in the 60% band in 2024/25 and 2.29m losing some or all personal allowance by 2028/29, with thresholds frozen to April 2031. No budgeting app models it. This matches what Flump already has (take-home engine, £100k calculator, `packages/finance-engine`), so it is the lead opportunity.
2. **No UK competitor combines take-home, marginal-rate, pension, debt and a self-employed ledger.** UK budgeters (Emma, Snoop) ignore tax; tax tools (Taxfix, Pie, FreeAgent, Coconut) file returns and do not plan; ProjectionLab models UK tax well but is a scenario sandbox with no ledger. This is an inference from the sources, not a proven absence.
3. **The UK consumer-aggregator field is thinning.** Money Dashboard closed on 31 October 2023; Moneyhub is reported to have closed its consumer app in August 2026 (one source, UNVERIFIED). That creates demand for portability and export, but the closures show users care about getting their data out, not that they would choose Flump for it.
4. **MCP and LLM access is now a feature in the US, mostly unofficial or read-only.** Supply is rich (10+ community MCP servers for YNAB, Actual and Monarch; none official). Demand for MCP specifically is unproven. UK AI-trust surveys favour AI as an explainer over the user's own numbers, not as an adviser. This is both an opening and a regulatory risk.
5. **Regulation creates timing and constraints.** MTD for Income Tax is live since April 2026 (phase 2 in April 2027), but submission needs HMRC software recognition. FCA targeted support is a regulated activity live since April 2026; Flump must stay on the guidance side. Inheritance tax on pensions starts April 2027.
6. **Recommended top five** (after an independent opus review of the draft ranking, section 5): (1) tax-year rule versioning plus a marginal-rate what-if engine, (2) export plus CSV import, (3) scoped read-first tax-aware MCP tools, (4) UK retirement and pension projection including IHT from 2027, (5) an MTD discovery spike. A compliance gate (perimeter opinion, advice-language review, MCP DPIA) runs alongside 1 and 3 and must land before the friends beta.
7. **Biggest gap: no direct evidence of demand for Flump itself.** The friends beta should be instrumented to answer that (section 7).

### Gap analysis (inference)

1. Whole-life take-home and marginal-rate planning against real balances: not offered together by any tool reviewed.
2. Joined-up pension, mortgage, debt and savings in GBP: ProjectionLab is closest but has no ledger.
3. Self-employed and rental ledger linked to personal net worth and tax-year position: MTD products are business accounting.
4. Portability and shutdown resilience: two UK consumer closures.
5. First-party scoped UK-aware MCP: existing routes are unofficial or read-only betas.
6. UK paid tiers cluster at about £4 to £15 a month; MTD software £15 to £35. A free tier plus transparent pricing is an anchor, not a differentiator.

## 8. Proposed epics

Format follows [audit/EPICS.md](../audit/EPICS.md). Numbers are provisional.

## E25. UK tax-year rules and marginal-rate what-if planner

- **Severity:** P1 for direction (lead differentiator; correctness is a launch gate).
- **Blocked by:** E4 (owner sign-off of tax vectors) and E22 (tax-year maintenance) for any shipped figure; design can start now.
- **Done when:** every tax rule is versioned by tax year with a source URL and test vector; one engine answers "what happens to my take-home if my gross changes by X" across the £100k taper, High Income Child Benefit Charge, salary sacrifice (including the April 2029 £2,000 cap), Plan 2 and Plan 5 student loans, and Scottish bands; marginal-rate and cliff views shown on the home and calculator pages with an "as at tax year" label; marketing calculators and the app share one engine; all copy is guidance, not advice.
- **Notes:** the lead opportunity (backlog #1). Evidence: HMRC-derived counts of 698,000 in the 60% band and 2.29m by 2028-29. Do not model a household-basis child benefit charge or any Budget 2026 change until confirmed on gov.uk. Scotland is a correctness requirement, not a demand-led feature.
- **Labels:** needs-triage

## E26. Tax-aware agent tools (extension of E17)

- **Severity:** P2 (differentiator; depends on E25 and E29).
- **Blocked by:** E17 design, E25 engine, E29 compliance gate.
- **Done when:** MCP tools and prompts expose the E25 engine ("what if I sacrifice £X", "show my marginal rate", "explain my take-home") with read-only default scopes, explicit consent for any write, an audit log and per-tool descriptions worded as guidance; an eval harness proves models select tools correctly and do not output personal product recommendations; a DPIA covers disclosure to the model provider and transfers; marketing claims match what ships.
- **Notes:** supply side is crowded with unofficial community servers and none is official, but demand for MCP specifically is unproven; measure beta usage before investing beyond the first tools. Fold into E17 if the owner prefers one epic.
- **Labels:** needs-triage

## E27. UK retirement and pension projection

- **Severity:** P2.
- **Blocked by:** E4 vectors for pension tax rules; E29 for projection wording.
- **Done when:** projection shows contributions, tax relief, UFPLS and PCLS options, state pension and the effect of inheritance tax on unused pensions from 6 April 2027, with assumptions visible and editable; manual pension entry with links out to MoneyHelper, no dashboard connection claimed; self-employed saving gap shown for self-employed users.
- **Notes:** ProjectionLab already models UK UFPLS and PCLS, so Flump's angle is the link to take-home and the ledger, not scenario depth. Pensions Dashboards public launch is expected in FY2027-28 (UNVERIFIED), and third-party access is unlikely.
- **Labels:** needs-triage

## E28. MTD for Income Tax discovery spike

- **Severity:** P3 (spike with a dated decision).
- **Blocked by:** none for discovery; a submission build needs HMRC recognition work.
- **Done when:** a written decision before April 2027 on one of three paths: (a) record and quarterly estimate with export to bridging software only, (b) HMRC-recognised submission, (c) do nothing; evidence from at least five beta self-employed or landlord users; the ledger's labels never claim "MTD-compliant" unless the digital-links and fraud-prevention requirements are met.
- **Notes:** segment size is verified (2.88m landlords; MTD live April 2026, phase 2 April 2027), but no direct demand for Flump was found and the market is crowded and cheap (FreeAgent free with some bank accounts). Scope counts in press conflict; use gov.uk or ICAEW.
- **Labels:** needs-triage

## E29. Advice-boundary and AI compliance gate

- **Severity:** Critical for the friends beta (gates E25 copy and E26).
- **Blocked by:** owner and legal input.
- **Done when:** a lawyer's FCA perimeter opinion covers projections, salary-sacrifice and pension "optimise" outputs and LLM replies through MCP; a copy review applies "guidance, not advice" wording and removes "you should" language on investments, pensions and mortgages; no segment-based nudges (targeted support is regulated); an MCP DPIA covers lawful basis for disclosure to the model provider, transfers, Article 9 inference, retention and no training on user data; Consumer Duty principles adopted voluntarily.
- **Notes:** extends E19, which currently asks for the perimeter view at launch; the research suggests it is needed before the friends beta. Targeted support went live 6 April 2026.
- **Labels:** needs-triage
