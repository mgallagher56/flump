Chapter 01

Character and voice

Flump is a calm guide for a typical UK household: someone renting, someone whose mortgage fix is ending, someone saving for a first home, or someone getting to grips with a pension. Many arrive worried about money, so screens and words avoid dread, clutter and judgement.

Visual character: Midnight and Mint

The light theme ("Mint") uses a pale mint canvas with deep-green ink and charcoal primary buttons. The dark theme ("Midnight") uses cool charcoal surfaces with mint headline figures, teal links and apricot for what you owe. The marketing site adds a lit orb, soft bubbles, glass and paper cards and calm motion; the apps stay quiet.

Three screen tests:

  1. Could a person who is worried about money read it without feeling judged or alarmed?
  2. Is there one thing to look at first?
  3. Would it look like the same product if it were moved from the marketing site to the app, or from the web to a phone?

Voice traits and tagline

Flump sounds like a calm, numerate friend who shows you the working: plain (everyday words, short sentences), factual (says what a number is and assumes, never recommends), even-handed (every situation has equal weight) and honest (says "estimate" when it is one).

Tagline: "Flump: finance without the fog" (sentence case, colon after the name, never a dash). It goes only in page metadata, the footer brand line, the share image and the web manifest description.

Full wording rules, glossary and advice boundary rules live in Flump copy guide.

Guidance, not advice, and no shaming

Flump gives facts and estimates, never recommendations. Every encouragement feature must pass all five E35 no-shaming tests (N1 to N5): no points, scores, grades, leagues or ranks; no comparison with other people; no loss-framed streaks; acknowledge the action, never the amount; and equal warmth for every starting point including debt, renting and low savings.

Sources: STYLE-GUIDE 1; UX-GUIDELINES 1, 9, 11; COPY-GUIDE 1, 2.1, 2.3, 2.5

Logo rules and what not to do

  • Clear space around the logo is at least half its width.

Colour

Three layers structure every colour: raw primitives, semantic tokens with a light and dark value, and component recipes. Components use only semantic tokens (Rule C1: no hex, rgb or primitive scale name in component or page code).

Light theme: Mint

About 60% mint canvas, 30% off-white cards and bands, 10% accent: solid mint hero, teal links and outline buttons, apricot for debt.

Chapter 04

Typography

Flump bundles two self-hosted variable typefaces through @repo/ui/fonts.css: Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and display, and Inter for body text, labels and tabular figures. No third typeface is used; system monospace is for code samples only. Headings widen word spacing by 0.12em on web because Plus Jakarta Sans has a narrow space at weights 600 and 700.

Typography rules

  • T1 to T3: All sizes come from text styles; 12px is the minimum floor; weights are 400, 500, 600 and 700 only.
  • T4: Every money amount, percentage, date, duration, table cell and slider value uses tabular-nums and en-GB formatting.
  • T5 to T9: Headline figure is the largest text after the h1 and "Not set" is never larger than a real value; sentence case everywhere (no uppercase eyebrows or caps tracking); one h1 per page with no skipped heading levels; body lines at most 75 characters wide; text survives 200% zoom without clipping.

Chapter 07

Illustration

Flump draws every picture from shared shape lists through the single Illustration primitive (Rule IL4). No external image files are used for illustrations: the web renders SVG from the shape list, and mobile draws the exact same list with react-native-svg via @repo/ui/illustration-data.

Document cards (27 November 2026)

Tilted bank cards, payslips, budget slips, statements, calendar pages and tickets (at most 6 degrees of tilt) drawn in a 300-unit grid and never narrower than 280px so the smallest text (13 grid units) stays at 12px or above. Cards with sample figures carry a real-text "Example" tag.

Rendered directly from documentDefinitions["empty-accounts"] in Illustration.documents.ts.

App palette and opt-in orb look

By default (palette="app"), credit cards stay mint (primary.subtle) and never rose or red. The marketing home page and the AI connection page's "What you see" picture may opt into palette="orb" under rule MC1.

Rendered directly from documentDefinitions["feature-debt"] in Illustration.documents.ts.

Flat scenes (E17)

Apps keep flat scenes on a 240 by 180 grid (ground line at 152, 20 clear at the sides, 10 at the top) until the switch to document cards (decision D4). Each scene uses one sun, one dotted arc, at most one leaf or sprout and three to five rounded solid forms (14px corners, no outlines).

Placement and exclusions

  • Pictures are decorative (aria-hidden); text beside them carries the meaning.
  • Never show beside a price on marketing pricing, beside a real figure on a compact viewport, inside a notice, or in an error about money. Error pages keep their picture still.
  • Never draw people, faces, piles of coins, upward-arrow growth clichés, red fills, chequered finish flags or glass objects inside pictures.

Chapter 08

Motion

Flump has two motion settings (27 November 2026). In the web and mobile apps, people read their own money, so motion stays quiet, explains state changes and never moves a figure while it is read. On the marketing site, calm scroll-triggered and ambient scenes show how Flump works using sample figures. GSAP and three.js are restricted strictly to apps/marketing (Rule AT4).

Chapter 09

Charts

Chart colours come only from chart.* via chartColor() and seriesColor() (Rule CH1). Colour identifies an entity, never an outcome (CH2): an account keeps its assigned colour across views, and debts use apricot (chart.debt), never red.

Chart rules

Structure and accessibility

  • C1: Every chart is a ChartFigure with a takeaway title, a visible one-sentence summary (at most 2 figures) and a "Show data" table.
  • C2: Choose form by question: line for change over time, sorted horizontal bars for parts of a whole or ranking, progress bar against a limit. Donuts hold at most 5 slices including "Other" (capSeriesWithOther).
  • C3 to C4: Label up to 3 series directly at line end; legend for 4. Never rely on colour alone: pair series with a dash, marker or direct label.
  • C8 to C9: Tap or keyboard focus selects a point with a fixed readout; arrow keys step through points. Animation respects useChartAnimation (off under reduced motion and while dragging a slider).

Axes, estimates and ticks

  • C5: Forecasts are dashed (chart.projection), state assumptions in a caption beneath, and forecasts over 1 year show a range fill (chart.band) with low and high pound values in text.
  • C6 to C7: Bar axes start at zero; no log scales or second y-axis. At most 6 x-axis ticks; years as "2030" (never "Year 5"); short months always three letters ("Sep", never "Sept").
  • CH5: Axis, legend and tooltip figures use tabular numerals and formatAxisCurrency(). Unknown values show as "Not set", never plotted as zero.
  • C12: Hover tooltips use ChartTooltipContent with dashed chart.baseline line cursor or soft chart.grid bar band.

Chapter 10

Do and do not

A quick checklist drawn directly from the standing rules in earlier chapters.

Tokens and colours (C1, C2, C9)

Do

Use semantic tokens only, keep debt in apricot (chart.debt) and negative balances in text.primary, and use mint as a fill in light.

Do not

Hardcode hex or rgb values, use red or warning badges on ordinary debt, or use mint as text on a light background.

Orb palette discipline (MC1, MC2)

Do

Keep violet and rose tokens on orb-like elements, the allow-listed home page marks and the area map tiles.

Do not

Use violet or rose as a band background, tint or card material, or pair an orb colour with debt, gain or loss.

Voice and copy (COPY-GUIDE 2.3, 5.1, 5.4)

Do

Write plain en-GB in sentence case, keep sentences under 25 words, label estimates clearly and tag illustrative figures as Example.

Do not

Use em dashes, exclamation marks, ampersands in prose, hype words, judgements or target figures chosen for the reader.

Saved figures vs scenarios (S1 to S8)

Do

Keep headline figures and KpiStrips driven by saved data, and isolate what-if controls inside ScenarioZone labelled Illustration, not saved.

Do not

Mix scenario values into saved KPIs, put sliders outside ScenarioZone, or offer an Apply button that overwrites saved data.

Typography and numbers (T1 to T4)

Do

Use Plus Jakarta Sans for headings, Inter for body and tabular-nums with en-GB formatting on every figure.

Do not

Go below 12px, use weights above 700, use uppercase eyebrows, or use type="number" on money and percentage fields (F2).

Motion and platforms (AT1 to AT4, MK1, M4)

Do

Swap or cross-fade real figures in the apps, keep GSAP and three.js inside apps/marketing, and honour prefers-reduced-motion everywhere.

Do not

Count up or roll real balances, put WebGL or parallax in the apps, or hide content until JavaScript runs.

Logo and illustrations (STYLE-GUIDE 8.3, 9)

Do

Keep half-width clear space around the K2 logo, store pictures as shape lists (IL4) and keep a real-text Example tag on sample cards.

Do not

Place the logo on surface.hero or photographs, recolour or rotate it, or draw faces, coin piles or glass objects inside pictures.
