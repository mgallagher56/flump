Joint items (row 2): a user can mark an account, a debt or a budget line as joint and store their own percentage share (also their share of their home, `property_ownership_share`), and a preference (`partner_mode`) shows those controls. No second person is identified, linked or invited, and no partner name, email or income is asked for; the other holder is a data subject only if the user types their name into free text. The whole balance of a joint account sits on the one user's account and figures count it at their share.

Households (row 13): built behind a server-side switch that is off everywhere, including Railway. Only the owner turns it on, once the specialist charity's review (CD7), the owner's signature on the household DPIA (`docs/legal/dpia-household.md`) and the owner's approval of the help line wording (CD10) all exist. Each person is a data subject in their own right and Flump is the controller for both. Leaving or deleting an account removes that person's membership at once, closes the household and tells the other person nothing; it never deletes the other person's data. The public privacy notice is not changed while the switch is off.

Not done: no marketing email, no advertising, no analytics cookies, no profiling, no automated decisions with legal or similar effect, no bank connection (`BANK_CONNECTIONS_ENABLED` is not set on the backend). Cookieless page counts on the marketing site exist in code and are off (`ANALYTICS_MODE` is not set).

## How retention is applied

The periods above are policies in `apps/backend/src/jobs/retention.ts`. Since 25 November 2026 (D5 revised) the backend runs them itself, a few minutes after each start and then daily, behind a lock so that several instances run them once; it also sweeps expired request counters every 15 minutes. The run is recorded (times and counts only) in `retention_runs`, and `/health/ready` shows its age. `RETENTION_SCHEDULE_ENABLED=false` stops it. A Railway cron service running `bun run retention:purge` is an optional second trigger; none exists today. The project was created on 3 November 2026, so no period had lapsed before this change. **Until it is deployed and a run is confirmed, the timed lines rely on this code being live by 3 December 2026.** The backup job and its 35-day bucket rule in `docs/runbooks/backup-restore.md` do not exist yet either. Rollback copies a migration keeps (`liability_sign_backfill`, `invoice_tax_record_category_backfill`, which holds tax record ids and their earlier category, and the like) are kept up to 90 days by the same job and are not in the export.

## Security measures (summary)

Signed tokens checked on every request; one user's rows are keyed by user id; bank tokens and receipt files are encrypted in the application before storage; MCP tokens are stored as hashes; sessions last 14 days at most; sensitive actions need a recent sign-in; rate limits; an append-only audit trail; error reports and logs scrubbed of personal data. Detail: `docs/audit/D-security.md` and the security plans.
