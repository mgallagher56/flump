# Flump UX guidelines

Status: adopted 2026-11-14. Applies to the marketing site, the web app and the future mobile app.

These are the standing rules for how Flump behaves. How it looks is in [STYLE-GUIDE.md](./STYLE-GUIDE.md). What is shared across platforms is in [CROSS-PLATFORM.md](./CROSS-PLATFORM.md). The evidence behind the rules is in [RESEARCH-2026-11.md](./RESEARCH-2026-11.md), and how the product measures up today is in [UX-ASSESSMENT-2026-11.md](./UX-ASSESSMENT-2026-11.md).

## How to read this document

- **Rule ids** are stable (`H1`, `F3`). Cite them in reviews.
- **Check** says how each rule is held: **Enforced by** a named law or test; **Proposed check** for a check that does not exist yet (described in the assessment, section 6); or **Review only**.
- **[PR n]** means the check or component arrives with that PR of the polish plan ([`docs/design/e16-polish/PLAN.md`](../design/e16-polish/PLAN.md)). The polish plan's principles P1 to P13 are the acceptance criteria for that work; this document restates the lasting ones as product rules and adds what the plan does not cover. Where both exist they agree, and the plan's id is given.
- **Do and don't** examples are from our own screens on `main` at 2026-11-14 (frames in `docs/design/e16-polish/before/`, findings in the polish audit).
- **Wording is not set here.** Words belong to the copy guide ([`docs/copy/COPY-GUIDE.md`](../copy/COPY-GUIDE.md)) and to the [advice boundary policy](../compliance/advice-boundary-policy.md) (rules R1 to R8). Where a rule below touches wording it points there.
- AGENTS.md laws and owner decisions in `docs/audit/plans/DECISIONS.md` outrank this document.

## 1. Principles

Who we design for: a typical UK household. About half of UK adults find everyday percentages hard, about a third read charts with difficulty, and people look at their money less when they expect bad news. Many will arrive worried.

| #   | Principle                               | What it means in practice                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **One answer per screen.**              | Every page answers one question with one headline figure. Everything else supports it or waits behind a disclosure.                                                    |
| 2   | **The interface does the sums.**        | Nobody should have to add, subtract, convert a rate or compare two numbers in their head. Show the pound figure and the difference.                                    |
| 3   | **Never judge.**                        | No colour, icon, comparison or ordering that implies a person's finances are bad. Renting, owing money and a negative net worth are ordinary situations. (Owner rule.) |
| 4   | **Saved facts and what-ifs never mix.** | What the user has told us is shown as fact. Anything they are trying out is clearly an illustration and never changes the facts. (Owner rule.)                         |
| 5   | **Honest about uncertainty.**           | An estimate is labelled as one, shows its assumptions and, where it looks ahead more than a year, shows a range.                                                       |
| 6   | **Calm by default.**                    | Quiet notices, no alarm colours for ordinary states, no streaks or rewards, no notifications the user did not ask for.                                                 |
| 7   | **Works for everyone.**                 | WCAG 2.2 AA on every surface, keyboard and screen reader first-class, no meaning carried by colour, hover or drag alone.                                               |
| 8   | **The same product everywhere.**        | The same names, order, figures, colours and states on marketing, web and mobile. Only platform chrome differs.                                                         |

Rationales in this document name a mechanism (working memory of about four items, target size, response time, avoidance). They do not cite "Miller's law", "Hick's law" or "the Doherty threshold": the research shows those are over-claimed as interface rules.

| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| E1  | A scenario result updates within 0.1 seconds of an input changing, with no spinner. The result is announced politely to screen readers without moving focus.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |                                               |                                                                                   | **Enforced by** the calculators' live-region test; **Review only** for timing                                    |

## 7. Destructive and irreversible actions

Deletes in Flump are permanent (owner decision E5 D4-A: no soft delete, no undo). So they are confirmed.

| Id  | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Do                                    | Don't                                    | Check                                                                                       |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D1  | Every permanent delete goes through `ConfirmDialog`: the title names the thing, the body states the consequence, the destructive button says what it does, and "Cancel" is the safe default focus.                                   | "Delete Barclaycard?" / "Delete debt" | "Are you sure?" / "OK"; `window.confirm` | **Enforced by** `ConfirmDialog` tests, and Biome `noAlert` (no `window.confirm` or `alert`) |
| D2  | Do not confirm actions that can be undone or redone cheaply (closing a drawer, resetting a scenario, changing a filter). Confirmation is kept rare so it is read.                                                                    | "Reset to saved figures" acts at once |                                          | **Review only**                                                                             |
| D3  | The destructive control is not the primary button, is not next to the primary button without separation, and uses `danger` styling only inside the confirmation.                                                                     | Delete in a row menu                  | A red "Delete" beside "Save"             | **Review only**                                                                             |
| D4  | Anything that changes saved financial data shows the values before saving (an edit form, a review step) or can be corrected straight after. Imports and bulk changes always have a review step.                                      | Import review, commit and undo        |                                          | **Review only**                                                                             |

| Id  | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Check                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## 14. Changing these rules
