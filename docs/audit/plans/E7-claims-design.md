# E7 claims design: claim register

Status: design input for E7 (marketing and security claims vs reality). Nothing here is approved wording until the owner fills in the "Approved wording (owner)" column.

## Owner decisions applied

From `docs/audit/plans/DECISIONS.md` (main checkout) and the E7 draft plan:

- **D0:** the owner signs off all marketing copy. Regulatory statements (FCA, GDPR) are only added with the owner's explicit wording, never drafted as fact. Rows marked O+L also need legal sign-off.
- **D1-b:** public marketing and the web public home describe bank sync as "coming soon"; remove the comparison-matrix and pricing rows. In-app dialogs use the beta token wording (the token flow exists).
- **Pricing:** not set. The pricing page keeps its full structure (cards, features, call to action) with an obvious placeholder price. This supersedes D2-a (single free card). No trial or Pro claims that nothing implements.
- **D4:** the fake enquiry form is removed; an obvious placeholder email (`security@example.invalid`) is used for the security contact and manual account-closure route.
- **D6:** placeholder company name, number and address in the footer, never invented real details.
- **D5-c:** MCP is labelled "preview"; npx and remote instructions and URLs are removed; the tool catalogue stays.
- **D7-a:** the MCP "strict PII anonymisation" wording (A4) is folded into E7.

All placeholders live in `apps/marketing/src/utils/placeholders.ts`. Replace each before launch (grep `PLACEHOLDER` or `example.invalid`).

Legend: **O** owner, **O+L** owner plus legal, **R** remove rather than reword. The "Proposed wording" column is the draft; where D1-b, the pricing decision or D5-c differ from it, the decision wins.

**What E1 and E3 changed:**

- Auth is now real (WorkOS JWT verifier). "Your data is private to your account" is now defensible.
- Bank tokens are encrypted with a required key and are no longer returned to clients.
- Database TLS verifies certificates by default.
- E1 removed the MCP OAuth metadata route. The remote MCP claims became more false, not less.

Approver codes: **O** is the owner. **O+L** is the owner plus legal (FCA status, UK GDPR, consumer pricing, company identity). **R** means remove the element rather than reword it.
