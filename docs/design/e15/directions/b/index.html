Token sketch

Palette primitives mapped to the repo semantic names. Values below are read live from the CSS custom properties of both themes. Dark is the default on bare :root.

TokenDarkLight

Radius scale

2 bars and thumbs, 4 default (controls, panels, chips), 6 overlays, 8 device frame only. No pills.

Elevation

No shadows. Depth is step-up surface plus a 1px hairline: canvas, sunken, card, subtle, overlay. Focus is a 2px ring with 2px offset.

Spacing and density

4px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24. Panel header 36px, KPI cell 14/16 padding. Controls 34px on desktop, 44px under 720px container width. Gutters 24px wide, 16px narrow. Tables 6px row padding.

Motion

90 to 140ms, linear only (colour and chevron rotation). Live dots use steps(2) blink at 1.2s. No entrance animation, no easing curves. Everything off under prefers-reduced-motion.

Chart palette

Cyan assets and net worth, amber liabilities and balances, violet for the user's own scenario. Never red for debt reduction. Dashed means projection or alternative.

Rationale and trade-offs

  • Every number traceable to inputs. Hairline grids, axis ticks on every month and year, and mono tabular numerals make each figure scannable and comparable. KPI cells show their working: a split bar for outgoings against surplus, a month meter for the buffer, a real sparkline for net worth only (no invented trends for other KPIs).
  • Show the next action. The coach card carries a 3px primary rule and breaks the sentence into three stat chips, so the saving is readable before the prose.
  • Calm by default, detail on demand. Dense, but progressive: the schedule sits in a closed drawer, the mortgage controls fold into a summary bar plus Adjust at phone width, and hover crosshairs reveal month values.
  • Build cost across 12 screens: M. Colour tokens are an S swap. The M comes from re-skinning type (mono numerals and caps captions everywhere), a new KPI cell with micro-visuals, and a chart component with crosshair grid, dashed projections and annotations (L on its own). Sharp radii and no shadows simplify Panda recipes rather than complicate them.
  • Mobile fit. Dashboard KPIs become a 2x2 compact grid (chosen over a scroll strip so all four figures are seen at once). A 5-tab bar replaces the top nav, controls reach 44px, charts redraw at true pixel width so axis text stays 12px.
  • Risks. Dark-first density can feel cold or intimidating for a nervous first-time user; the amber for liabilities is not a conventional colour for debt and needs the "owed" wording to carry meaning; heavy mono use reduces warmth, so copy must stay plain-spoken. Light theme is fully designed but is the secondary experience.
