        Rationale and trade-offs

        Fit with principles

        • Every number traceable to inputs. Captions sit directly under figures and charts and restate the arithmetic in plain words ("rose from £84,300 to £97,750"), and the serif figures are the largest thing on screen so the eye lands on the number, then its source.
        • Show the next action. The coach card is the only tinted block on the dashboard, placed straight after the KPIs, with a single primary button.
        • Calm by default, detail on demand. Rules and whitespace group content instead of boxes; schedules live in a collapsed drawer; the mobile mortgage controls collapse behind Adjust.

        Build cost across 12 screens

        M. The semantic token remap is small and the layout primitives stay. The cost is the serif display face (font loading, a numeric style variant for KPI, chart axis and table figures) and restyling cards to rules, which touches every screen once but changes no behaviour.

        Mobile fit

        KPI strip becomes a 2x2 grid with rules, tab bar replaces the top nav, mortgage controls collapse to a summary bar with Adjust, charts redraw with fewer ticks. All controls are 44px or taller.

        Risks

        • Hairline borders are quiet in bright sunlight; input borders are held at 3:1 to compensate.
        • Amber for debt is calmer than red but signals urgency less; the 27.9% APR card needs explicit text and ordering, not colour.
        • Webfont swap can shift KPI widths; self-hosting with a size-adjusted fallback is advised.
        • Fewer boxes means less obvious tap targets for widgets, so each carries a visible text link.
