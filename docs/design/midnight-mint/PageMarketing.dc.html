How it works

Three steps, about ten minutes a month.

1

Add your accounts

Savings, pensions, a mortgage, cards and loans. Type in the balances yourself.

2

Check in once a month

Flump walks you through each account. Update the balance, or say nothing changed.

3

See your whole picture

Net worth, budget, debt and savings on one page, with the month before for comparison.

Net worth

One figure for everything you own and owe.

Each check-in adds a point to your history, so you can see how the figure has moved month by month.
