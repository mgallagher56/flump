Five choices to make: an accent colour, a new "Finish setting up" row, where illustrations go, which small animations to add, and how sections and results stand apart. Every option is drawn with today's tokens so you can compare like with like.

3. Illustrations

Scenes earn a place where a page has nothing of the user's to show yet, or at the start and end of a short task. They never sit beside figures. Four new scenes are drawn below in the Budget scene's style. Two quick sketches follow, so every setup step has one.

How far to go

3A. The "now" set Recommended

Savings, debts, pension and the check-in get their own scene, and the marketing not-found page reuses the signpost. Five places, four drawings.

3B. Every empty state

3A plus mortgage, Self Assessment, forecast, take-home pay and the onboarding steps. About ten drawings, and the app never repeats a scene.

3C. Tidy up only

No new drawings. Keep the four scenes that exist and accept that savings, debts and pension share the accounts scene.

Style sheet for the scene family

Grid. 240 by 170. Everything stands on the ground line at 152. Keep 20 clear at the sides and 10 at the top. The sun sits in a top corner, 36 to 40 across with a halo of about 1.7 times that.

Deep teal: the main object, one per scene Pale teal: its partner or its back Sage: the third form, stems and leaves Sun: one disc and its halo, muted Sand: the ground, nothing else Paper: small details on teal

Colour. Five fills and paper, all from tokens, so dark mode comes free. If an accent is chosen in section 1, it colours one small shape per scene (a day on the calendar, a coin) and nothing more.

  • Shapes. Rounded rectangles with a 14 corner, circles, and the leaf (a quarter-round with a point). No outlines.
  • Three lines only. The dotted arc (3 wide, dots 10 apart), the stem (4) and the pole (5), all with round ends.
  • One of each. One sun, one dotted arc, at most one leaf or sprout. Three to five solid forms.
  • No people, faces, coins with symbols, arrows or red. Nothing that grades a situation.
  • Mobile. Keep each scene as a list of shapes (kind, position, colour role) in one shared file. The web draws it as SVG; the mobile app draws the same list with its own SVG library. No image files.

4. Micro-animations

Ten small demos. Press Play on each. Five are marked for a first pass; the rest wait or are left out. Everything plays once, in answer to something the user did, and nothing loops. Turn on "Reduced motion" in the top bar and play them again to see what people who ask for less motion get.

Proposed additions to the motion tokens: durations.draw 600ms, durations.scene 900ms and easings.settle cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), a soft landing with no bounce. Everything else uses the existing fast 120ms, base 200ms, slow 320ms, standard, enter and exit.

What changes

  • Every section opens with an icon tile, a title and one line saying what it is.
  • Gaps between sections grow from 20 to 36.
  • The dashed outline rises from 1.3:1 to 3.6:1 against the canvas, so it can be seen.
  • The result sits in its own card with a figure at 36, to the right of the inputs on desktop and under them on a phone.

The rule for every page

  • Saved sections are cards on the canvas. A modelling zone is the only dashed outline on a page and has no fill.
  • A zone holds exactly one result card. Nothing else inside a zone is a card.
  • The result card never uses surface.hero or a tint.

Components that change

  • ScenarioZone: border goes from border to border.input; gains a result slot.
  • New ResultBlock in packages/ui: label, lead-in, figure, note, one supporting row.
  • SectionHeader (icon tile, title, description) and the section gap in PageBody.
  • Each calculator swaps its own outcome markup for ResultBlock.

Summary

Recommended combination

  • Accent A, Damson, with D's bolder tints alongside it
  • Setup 2A, the ring and next step, borrowing 2C's finish state
  • Scenes 3A: the four "now" scenes and the style sheet
  • Motion: demos 1, 3, 4, 5 and 7
  • Sections 5A: headers and a result card, on every page

Plum is the one free hue that is warm, sits well against teal and cannot be read as good or bad. The ring row says what to do next in one line. The scenes and five animations add life only where the user has just done something or has nothing yet.

Cost to build

  • PR 1, tokens: the accent group, three motion tokens, contrast tests, native tokens regenerated. Touches packages/tokens, the Panda preset in packages/ui, AGENTS.md section 2 and the style guide.
  • PR 2, scenes: four scenes and the accent shape in packages/ui Illustration; empty states and check-in in apps/web.
  • PR 3, setup row: a small ProgressRing in packages/ui; SetupChecklist in apps/web; "Not for me" needs one saved preference.
  • PR 4, motion: the five first-pass animations in packages/ui and the check-in.
  • PR 5, sections: ResultBlock, SectionHeader, ScenarioZone and PageBody in packages/ui; every page with a zone in apps/web.
  • Later, one PR: marketing scenes and the second-pass motion.

Five PRs now, one later. Choosing D alone takes the accent tokens out of PR 1.

What affects the mobile app

  • Accent and motion tokens reach mobile through the generated native tokens, so they must land there in PR 1.
  • Scenes should be stored as shape lists, not web-only SVG, so mobile can draw the same ones.
  • The ring, the setup row, the result block and the section header join the parity matrix in CROSS-PLATFORM.
  • Demo 3 uses the platform's own screen transition on mobile (rule M6); the other four can match the web timings.
  • Mobile must honour the system reduce-motion setting in the same way.

Rules that would change

  • accent becomes a seventh colour namespace.
  • M5 gains one line: the setup finish state is allowed.
